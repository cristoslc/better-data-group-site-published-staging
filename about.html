About

Work directly with the person doing the research.

Kwong Chan

Kwong connects market research to product and launch decisions, across early market studies for startups and the data behind global product launches.

He is the Executive Director of the DMSB AI Strategic Hub and an Academic Specialist in Marketing at Northeastern University's D'Amore-McKim School of Business, where he helped pioneer analytics education and won teaching awards for his methods. Before that he was an Associate Director in the Telecommunications and Technology practice at Nielsen, and a marketing professor at UMass Amherst.

His research spans innovation and analytics, published in IEEE Transactions on Engineering Management, the Journal of Product Innovation Management, the Journal of International Marketing, the Journal of Sports Management, the Journal of Public Policy & Marketing, and the Journal of Advertising. He holds a PhD in Marketing from Michigan State University and a Bachelor of Commerce from the University of Tasmania.

He has worked with companies including Samsung, Goodyear, VISA, Nike, ASUS, ACER, and General Motors.
