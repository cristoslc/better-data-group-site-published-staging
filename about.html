About us

Three people with experience in market research, innovation, and engineering.

01

Kwong Chan

From sparse market details for startups to fat numbers for a global product launch, Kwong links market evidence to the decisions teams make. He holds a PhD from Michigan State University in Marketing and a Bachelor of Commerce from the University of Tasmania, Australia.

Formerly an Associate Director for the Technology and Telecommunication industries at Nielsen, he is now at the DMSB AI Strategic Hub (DASH) at Northeastern University, helping build new visions in market research, design thinking, and sentiment analysis, and connecting students and industry collaborators to where AI meets business.

02

Rosanna Garcia

Rosanna studies how consumers adopt electric vehicles and presents her findings on the unique challenge of resistant innovations and the role of consumers in the sharing economy. In 2014 she launched her book Creating and Marketing New Products and Services.

She holds several technology patents across several fields, including one in agent-based modeling, and is a professor of Marketing & Innovation at NC State University. She holds a PhD in Marketing from Michigan State University, an MBA from the University of Rochester, NY, and a BS in Chemical Engineering from UC Santa Barbara, CA.

03

Paul Rummel

Paul is an engineering consultant who has brought 29 years of RF application experience to the semiconductor industry and 10 years of agent-based modeling knowledge to marketing experts. He is the founder and president of the product data management company Softricity LLC, and holds 4 patents addressing the communications industry.

Paul holds a BS in Electronic Engineering from California Polytechnic State University and an M.S. in Electrical Engineering from Michigan State University.
