About

Find out if it will sell, before you build it.

Harvard Business School research puts the failure rate of new products near 95%, largely because customer needs go unstudied. Kwong Chan studies why customers resist, what they will pay, and which evidence changes the bet, so teams decide before they spend.

01

Where he works now

Kwong is the Executive Director of the DMSB AI Strategic Hub and an Academic Specialist in Marketing at Northeastern University's D'Amore-McKim School of Business. He helped pioneer analytics education there and has won teaching awards for his methods.

02

Where he worked before

Kwong was an Associate Director in the Telecommunications and Technology practice at Nielsen, and a marketing professor at UMass Amherst.

03

What he has published

His research spans innovation and analytics, published in:

  • IEEE Transactions on Engineering Management
  • Journal of Product Innovation Management
  • Journal of International Marketing
  • Journal of Sports Management
  • Journal of Public Policy & Marketing
  • Journal of Advertising

He holds a PhD in Marketing from Michigan State University and a Bachelor of Commerce from the University of Tasmania.
