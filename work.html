Our Work

Three service areas, one discipline: connect the evidence to the decision, and say clearly when the evidence is thin.

New Products

We help teams develop new products more effectively and analyze markets to help companies overcome resistance to new innovations.

In practice, that means studying who resists the product, why, and what would change their minds — before you spend the launch budget learning it the expensive way.

Fat and Thin Data

Make sense of what customer opinions do to pricing and demand. Figure out what bad data in online opinions means for consumer decisions. Predict customer frustration before it happens. Discover what makes customers happy around the world for global brands.

"Fat data" is the deep, reliable set you can bet a launch on. "Thin data" is the noisy online stream everyone wants to use for free. We show you what each one can honestly support.

What's Hot

Our proprietary "lheir" engine plucks out unwanted online weeds before they make it into your carefully cultivated garden.

That is: it separates the online chatter worth trusting from the chatter that would poison a dashboard, a forecast, or a customer-care response.
