Our work

New Products

We study why customers resist new products and what could change their minds.

Resistant customers are not a marketing problem only. Who resists, why, and what would change their minds is learnable before the launch budget starts learning it the expensive way.

02

Fat and Thin Data

We study what customer opinions do to pricing and demand.

Figure out what bad data in online opinions means for consumer decisions. Study customer opinions for signs of frustration before customers say it out loud. Discover what makes customers happy around the world for global brands.

"Fat data" is the deep, reliable set you can bet a launch on. "Thin data" is the noisy online stream everyone wants to use for free. We show you what each one can honestly support.
