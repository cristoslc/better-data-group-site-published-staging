Services

01

New Products

Understand what holds customers back.

A useful product can still be hard to sell. We study who resists it, why, and what could change their minds. Use those findings to decide what to change in the product or how you bring it to market, before you commit your launch budget.

02

Fat and Thin Data

What do customer opinions tell you about price and demand?

Customer opinions can help explain what people value, what frustrates them, and how they view a price. We examine those opinions and the quality of the data behind them, so you can assess what they mean for pricing and demand.

We work with detailed research and online opinions. What matters is whether the evidence is fit for the decision.
